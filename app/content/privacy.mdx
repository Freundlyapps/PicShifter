# Privacy Policy

At PicShifter, we take your privacy seriously. Our service is designed with a privacy-first approach, ensuring your data remains secure and under your control. For a quick overview of our features, visit our [About page](/about).

## Data Processing

- All image processing is performed entirely in your browser
- We operate as a stateless service, meaning we don't store any images or personal data
- No cookies are used for tracking or advertising purposes
- No user accounts or registration required

For more details about our processing capabilities, check our [FAQ](/faq).

## Image Storage

- Images are processed in real-time and are never permanently stored on our servers
- All processing is temporary and exists only in your browser's memory
- Once processing is complete, all temporary data is automatically cleared
- We do not maintain any image history or backups

Learn more about our supported formats and processing limits in our [FAQ](/faq).

## Technical Data

We collect minimal technical data required for service operation:

- Basic usage statistics (anonymous)
- Error logs for service improvement
- No personal identification information is collected

## Third-Party Service

PicShifter is hosted on Netlify, which may collect certain data as part of its hosting services. 
Additionally, the Website may integrate with third-party tools, 
such as analytics services, to monitor site performance.

We recommend reviewing these policies for more information:

[Netlify Privacy Policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/)

[Google Analytics Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) 

For information about how to use our service within these privacy constraints, see our [Terms of Service](/terms).
